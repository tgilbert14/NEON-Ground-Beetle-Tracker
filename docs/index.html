What am I looking at?

Pitfall catch reflects beetle activity as well as local abundance. The explorer reports observed taxa and catch per valid trap-night; it does not estimate population density or site health. Sampled-bout detection frequency is not detection-corrected occupancy, and exploratory environmental associations are not causal evidence.
